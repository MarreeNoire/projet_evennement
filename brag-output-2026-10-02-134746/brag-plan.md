# Brag Plan: Event

## What is this app?
Event brings event discovery, ticketing, and the community around each gathering together.

## The angle
One event is more than a listing: it is a path from curiosity to a ticket to a conversation. Follow that path in Event’s warm, editorial interface, and land on the product’s own line: “Le fil des événements et des rencontres.”

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The Event mark draws together over warm paper. The line “Le fil des événements et des rencontres” settles beside it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A search for an event resolves into an event card and a clear ticket action.
- The ticket becomes a scannable QR pass, with the salon invitation alongside it.
- The salon UI opens on “Avant, pendant, après” and a conversation continues around the event.

## Outro / punchline
Return to the Event mark and the exact home-page promise: “Suis les échanges dans les salons et découvre les événements publiés sur Event.”

## User flow worth showing
Découvrir un événement → choisir son billet et obtenir son QR code → rejoindre le salon associé.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, tactile product film; warm paper and precise interface motion.
- Interpretation: spacious transitions, grounded UI details, restrained type, and only a few sound accents.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22.9 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #e4ddd1
- Accent: #60352c
- Text: #282622
- Display font: Inter (project font)
- Body font: Inter (project font)
- Strongest visual element: the custom Event ticket mark and ticket-shaped artwork.

## Share copy (draft)
Event relie la découverte, le billet et le salon qui prolonge la rencontre. Le fil des événements et des rencontres.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3; light, optimistic instrumental.
- Music treatment: start at 0s, sit quietly beneath the interface, fade gently at the 22.9s end.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`; the ticket transition lands at 8.74s and the salon opens at 13.11s, both strong cues. The 22.93s cue is beyond the 22.9s cut and is intentionally unused. Keep text holds ahead of the grid if readability needs it.
- Audio-reactive treatment: not implemented; the HyperFrames audio-analysis helper skill was unavailable in this runtime, so there is no claim that visuals react to the track.
- SFX posture: sparse; two or three quiet, motion-matched accents.
- Audio-coupled moments: a soft search interaction; the QR pass resolves on a musical accent; the salon arrives with a gentle reveal.
- Restraint rule: music supports the interface, never competes with copy or turns the UI into a sound-effect montage.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — La signature — 3.2s
Warm paper, an ink line draws the Event mark, and the product’s real home-page headline appears: “Le fil des événements et des rencontres.” Hold for reading.
Sequential/interaction: mark, then headline.
Audio intent: a gentle opening with a soft bed.
Audio-coupled idea: let the mark finish on an early beat; no per-letter typing.
Music: optimistic and restrained.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — La découverte — 5.5s
Recreate the home search field and one illustrative event card using the real “Rechercher un événement” and “Rechercher” UI copy. Card example “Festival des idées · Abidjan” is explicitly illustrative, not a real event claim. A small pointer selects “Choisir mes billets.”
Sequential/interaction: search field, then event card, then one clear button action.
Audio intent: build a little forward motion.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click for the search action; card appears on the 8.74s cue.
Music: continuous, low beneath legible interface copy.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Le billet — 4.3s
An illustrative ticket UI resolves into a high-contrast QR-like pattern; show “Accès à l’événement” with the explicit labels “EXEMPLE · NON VALIDE” and “EXEMPLE — NON SCANNABLE.” The pattern is decorative and cannot be used as a ticket.
Sequential/interaction: card turns into pass; status appears once.
Audio intent: a small confirmation, never a payment sound claim.
Audio-coupled idea: gentle drop as the pass settles.
Music: slight lift, no forced beat lock if it harms legibility.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Le salon — 5.5s
The ticket slides toward a compact, illustrative event salon UI. Use the exact product phrase “Avant, pendant, après” and a few fictional, generic conversation bubbles. A subtle orbit of attendee dots suggests a shared space without inventing platform metrics.
Sequential/interaction: salon title, then two message bubbles.
Audio intent: open the frame and make it feel welcoming.
Audio-coupled idea: salon opens at the 13.11s cue; quiet card-like movement.
Music: continuous, soft.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — La promesse — 4.4s
Return to the Event mark on warm paper. Hold the real supporting line: “Suis les échanges dans les salons et découvre les événements publiés sur Event.” End cleanly with generous whitespace.
Sequential/interaction: supporting line, then logo lockup.
Audio intent: resolve warmly.
Audio-coupled idea: let the logo settle naturally and fade; the 22.93s cue falls just outside the 22.9s cut.
Music: fade out through the last frame.
Transition mood: clean → end
